## Platform

## Purpose

SHUGE Odyssey is the owner's third personal-homepage edition: an ambitious, beautiful and responsive tiny-world experience that remains a clear introduction to the person and real projects. The owner explicitly requested a new independent GitHub Pages site, leaving older deployments untouched.

## Durable constraints

Use the current public V2 as a base and retain its improved touch mechanisms, creative labs, verified work links and readable HTML navigation. Public biography and contact fields were explicitly authorized by the owner; keep actual values in the page rather than copying the local personal configuration into project documents. Do not invent photos, anecdotes, credentials or achievements.

## Direction

A botanical planet and personal curiosity share one world. Pine night sky, porcelain surfaces, sage foliage and restrained apricot connect authored 3D materials with editorial Chinese content. Actual WebGL atmosphere/water/portal effects, cinematic movement and instanced detail create the visual ambition; static imagery does not substitute for the engine. The world can pause and simplify, while content and contact remain directly reachable.

## Publication and evidence

Only ndshuge/shuge-odyssey and its dist artifact are new publication targets. Original repositories and Sites remain preserved. Source/CPU checks, publication success and actual visual/device acceptance are separate evidence layers. Do not label historical V2 screenshots as V3 output or claim device smoothness without runtime measurement.
